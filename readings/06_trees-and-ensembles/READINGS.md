# Session 6 readings - Trees and ensembles

## Core

1. **Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman, _The Elements of Statistical Learning_, chapter 9.2
   (trees) and chapter 15 (random forests).**
   Free PDF: <https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/>
   The canonical treatment. Heavier than ISLR - read for the mechanism, not the notation.

2. **Breiman, "Random Forests", _Machine Learning_ 45, 2001.**
   <https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~breiman/randomforest2001.pdf>
   The original, and unusually readable. Section 2 on why decorrelating the trees is what
   makes the averaging pay is the part to concentrate on.

## Optional

3. **Chen & Guestrin, "XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System", KDD 2016.**
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.02754>
   How gradient boosting is actually implemented. Useful if your project uses tabular data,
   which most do.
