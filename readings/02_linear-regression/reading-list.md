# Session 2 readings - Linear regression

## Core

1. **ISLR chapter 3 ("Linear Regression").** <https://www.statlearning.com/>
   Sections 3.1-3.3 cover everything in the lecture plus the inferential material we will
   only touch on. Do the conceptual exercises 3.1-3.4.

2. **Çetinkaya-Rundel & Hardin, _Introduction to Modern Statistics_, the regression
   chapters.** <https://openintro-ims.netlify.app/>
   Free, R-based, and unusually good on reading a model's output honestly - which is what the
   `.Rmd` lab asks for.

## Optional

3. **Gelman, Hill & Vehtari, _Regression and Other Stories_ - examples and code.**
   <https://avehtari.github.io/ROS-Examples/>
   The complementary view: regression as a modelling craft rather than an algorithm. Skim the
   worked examples; the book itself is a good companion for the project.

## Bring to the session

Find a published regression result in your field and identify one coefficient whose
interpretation depends on the "holding the other columns fixed" clause. Be ready to say
whether that clause is plausible there.
