# Session 8 readings - Neural networks

## Core

1. **Nielsen, _Neural Networks and Deep Learning_, chapters 1-2.**
   Free online: <http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/>
   The clearest derivation of backpropagation written for people who have just met the chain
   rule. Chapter 2's four equations are exactly the lines in `demo.py`.

2. **Karpathy, "A Recipe for Training Neural Networks", 2019.**
   <http://karpathy.github.io/2019/04/25/recipe/>
   The debugging discipline, including "overfit a tiny batch first". Short, practical, and
   the source of the checklist in the lecture.

## Optional

3. **Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville, _Deep Learning_, chapter 6.**
   Free HTML: <https://www.deeplearningbook.org/>
   Feedforward networks in full, including the universal approximation results the lecture
   only names.

of handwritten notes (both sides), as announced.
