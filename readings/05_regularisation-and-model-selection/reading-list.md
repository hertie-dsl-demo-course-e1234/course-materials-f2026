# Session 5 readings - Regularisation and model selection

## Core

1. **ISLR chapters 5 and 6.** <https://www.statlearning.com/>
   Chapter 5 is resampling (cross-validation, the bootstrap); chapter 6 is subset selection,
   ridge and lasso. Between them they are the syllabus for this session.

2. **Raschka, "Model Evaluation, Model Selection, and Algorithm Selection in Machine
   Learning", 2018.** <https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12808>
   The most careful practical treatment of the hold-out / k-fold / nested-CV distinction
   available for free, and the source for the one-standard-error rule as we use it.

## Optional

3. **Hastie, Tibshirani & Wainwright, _Statistical Learning with Sparsity_, chapters 1-2.**
   Free PDF: <https://hastie.su.domains/StatLearnSparsity/>
   Where the lasso's exact zeros come from geometrically, and what happens with correlated
   predictors.
