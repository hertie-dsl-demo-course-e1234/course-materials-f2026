# Session 3 readings - Gradient descent

## Core

1. **Ruder, "An overview of gradient descent optimization algorithms", 2016.**
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.04747>
   The family tree - SGD, momentum, AdaGrad, RMSProp, Adam - in one readable survey. Read
   sections 1-4 now; the rest becomes useful in session 8.

2. **Goh, "Why Momentum Really Works", _Distill_, 2017.**
   <https://distill.pub/2017/momentum/>
   Interactive, and the fastest way to develop intuition for why a badly conditioned loss
   surface makes plain descent zig-zag. Play with the sliders; that is the reading.

## Optional

3. **Boyd & Vandenberghe, _Convex Optimization_, chapter 9.1-9.3.**
   Free PDF: <https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/>
   For the mathematically inclined: what convergence guarantees actually say, and why they
   stop applying in session 8.

## Bring to the session

Do the handout (`lectures/03_gradient-descent/handouts/`) before class - three descent steps
with a pen. The lab assumes you have.
