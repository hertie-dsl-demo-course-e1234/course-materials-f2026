# Session 10 readings - Evaluation and ethics

## Core

1. **Mitchell et al., "Model Cards for Model Reporting", 2018.**
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993>
   The documentation format your project must include. Two pages of substance; the rest is
   examples worth copying.

2. **Buolamwini & Gebru, "Gender Shades: Intersectional Accuracy Disparities in Commercial
   Gender Classification", FAT* 2018.**
   <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v81/buolamwini18a.html>
   The canonical demonstration that aggregate accuracy conceals subgroup failure - up to a
   34-point gap behind a single headline number.

## Optional

3. **Gebru et al., "Datasheets for Datasets", 2018.** <https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010>
   The dataset-side counterpart to a model card.

4. **Chouldechova, "Fair prediction with disparate impact", 2016.**
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.07524>
   The impossibility result: why calibration and equal error rates cannot both hold when base
   rates differ, stated precisely and in fifteen pages.

## Bring to the session

A draft model card for your project model, however rough. We will exchange and critique them
in pairs - the gaps other people find are the point.
