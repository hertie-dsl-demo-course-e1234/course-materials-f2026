# Session 1 readings - Introduction

All open access. Read the first item before the session; the other two are short and worth
the time.

## Core

1. **James, Witten, Hastie & Tibshirani, _An Introduction to Statistical Learning_,
   chapters 1-2.** Free PDF: <https://www.statlearning.com/>
   The reference text for sessions 1-7. Chapter 2 sets up the whole course: hypothesis
   families, loss, and the difference between training and test error. ~30 pages.

2. **Domingos, "A Few Useful Things to Know about Machine Learning", _CACM_ 55(10), 2012.**
   <https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~pedrod/papers/cacm12.pdf>
   Nine pages of hard-won practical judgement - "more data beats a cleverer algorithm",
   "feature engineering is the key", "overfitting has many faces". Re-read it in week 10 and
   you will get more out of it than you do now.

## Optional but recommended

3. **Breiman, "Statistical Modeling: The Two Cultures", _Statistical Science_ 16(3), 2001.**
   <https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1009213726>
   The founding argument for the predictive culture, and still the clearest statement of what
   the two traditions disagree about. Read the main text; the discussion pieces are optional.

## Bring to the session

One sentence: for a question you care about, is your interest prediction or explanation?
Session 1 argues those need different work, so be ready to defend your answer.
